What's inside

Nothing fancy. Just intentional training.

I'm not cramming 15 exercises into 25 minutes. Just the movements that matter, trained with intent. Quality over quantity.

01
The warm-up

8 to 10 minutes that open you up from the ankles to the hips. Part of the workout, not optional.

02
Workout 1 · Speed

Sprint drills done right, then 6 to 8 ramped 40 m sprints with full recovery.

03
Workout 2 · Upper power

Plyo push-ups, throws, explosive pulls and rotation, then heavy push and pull.

04
Workout 3 · Legs & core

Plyometrics, single-leg strength, posterior chain and deep core.

Why this works

Strong doesn't mean athletic.

I've seen guys move serious weight in the gym and still struggle to sprint, jump or change direction. Strength matters. A LOT. But a freak athlete trains all five of these.

01
Speed

The first thing we lose with age. The first thing we train.

02
Power

Force, produced fast. Upper body included.

03
Rotation

Force from your feet, through your hips, into your hands.

04
Stability

Single-leg strength and a core that holds you in place.

05
Deceleration

Stopping under control, where most injuries happen.
